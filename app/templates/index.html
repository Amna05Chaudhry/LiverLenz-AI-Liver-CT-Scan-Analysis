    About / How it works

    1

    Preprocess

    CT in Hounsfield units is clipped to an abdominal window (−100 to 250 HU) and resampled to 512×512. Each slice is stacked with its two neighbours (2.5-D input).

    2

    Find the liver

    A U-Net with a ResNet-18 encoder segments the liver on the whole slice (256×256). The largest 3-D component is kept.

    3

    Find tumours

    A second U-Net looks only inside a crop around the liver, at higher resolution, so small lesions keep their detail. Lesions are separated, boxed and measured.

    4

    Report & chat

    Findings come straight from the measurements. An LLM (Groq / Llama by default) writes the impression and answers questions; without an API key a template is used.

    Trained on the public LiTS dataset with a patient-wise split; held-out accuracy is reported in the project README. The model does not classify lesion type. By Amna Choudhri. Originally developed as a team university project; this version was refactored and extended by Amna Choudhri.
